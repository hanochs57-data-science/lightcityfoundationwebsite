Environmental / Societal Issues

Our Mission:
At our organization, we strive to build a healthier, cleaner, and more empowered community through impactful initiatives. To promote better health, we conduct monthly medical camps and health card camps, ensuring access to essential healthcare services for underprivileged families. We also provide ration kits to more than 400 families and arrange nutritious lunch for a month for around 130 people, supporting them in times of need. In addition, we distributed toothpaste, toothbrushes, combs, soaps, and powder to children, fostering good hygiene habits and instilling the value of self-care.
Our commitment extends beyond health and nutrition to creating a clean and sustainable environment. We organized the Milind Nagar Swachhta Abhiyan, encouraging residents to maintain cleanliness in their surroundings and take pride in a healthy neighborhood. On World Environment Day, we carried out a tree plantation program, raising awareness about environmental protection and the importance of greenery in urban areas.
We also work as a voice for the community by taking up local social issues. Several complaints were filed with the Assistant Commissioner of S Ward, ensuring that the challenges of Milind Nagar are addressed with urgency. These combined efforts reflect our dedication to creating lasting positive change.

Our Programmes :

  • Milind Nagar Swachta Abhiyan program to keep area clean

  • Tree plantation Program on Environment day

  • Filing complaints to Assistant Commissioner of S-ward related social issues of Milind Nagar

Empowering women and children through education, healthcare, and sustainable development initiatives.
