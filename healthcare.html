Our Aim:
In our organization, we believe that health and nutrition are the cornerstones of a dignified life. With this vision, we conduct monthly medical camps, bringing doctors and healthcare professionals closer to vulnerable communities who otherwise have limited access to treatment. Through our health card camps, we help families secure affordable healthcare facilities, reducing their financial burden during medical emergencies.

The struggles of daily sustenance, we have extended our support by distributing ration kits to more than 400 families, ensuring they do not go to bed hungry. To further strengthen food security, we have provided nutritious lunch for an entire month to nearly 130 individuals, giving them not just meals but also hope and relief.

In addition, we are dedicated to improving personal hygiene and healthy living among children. With this aim, we distributed toothpaste, toothbrushes, combs, soaps, and powder, instilling good hygiene practices from an early age. These small but significant steps not only safeguard children’s health but also empower them to live with confidence and dignity. Together, these initiatives reflect our commitment to creating healthier, happier, and more resilient communities.

Our Programmes:

  • E shram Card and Health Card Camp

  • Survey regarding rationing issues and made complaint against Ration shop vendor

  • Distribution of ration kits to 400 families

  • Distribution of toothpastes, toothbrush, comb, soap and powder to children

Empowering women and children through education, healthcare, and sustainable development initiatives.
