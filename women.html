    Women Empowerment

    Our Mission:
    Our mission is to envision and empower communities to take meaningful social action and create lasting change. We strive to equip young people with the knowledge and skills needed to make positive lifestyle choices and lead fulfilling lives.

    We are equally committed to supporting vulnerable women and children by providing access to counselling services, health and educational initiatives, and raising social and legal awareness. Through training, seminars, and counselling programs, we aim to build resilience, restore hope, and open pathways to a brighter, more secure future for all.

    Our Programmes:

    • Conducted Medical Camp every month

    • Conducted E shram Card and Health Card Camp

    • Conducted PAN CARD Camp

    • Electric Soldering training for women

    Empowering women and children through education, healthcare, and sustainable development initiatives.
